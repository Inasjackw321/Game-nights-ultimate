How scoring works

Every accepted answer has a tier: Common 100, Solid 200, Rare 350, Deep cut 500.

Those points are split by how many players gave the same answer. A deep cut nobody else said is worth 500. If five of you say "spaghetti", you get 20 each.

Typos are forgiven. If someone gives a real answer that isn't on the list, the host can approve it and it scores as Rare.
